# Rule: ground in facts

## The demand (word for word)

> You WILL ground your answer in 100% facts

## Good looks like

Context: asked how many people signed up last month.

1,240 people signed up in June 2026. This is the row count of the signups table for
that month.

## Bad looks like

Context: asked how many people signed up last month.

Probably a lot; signups usually climb around summer.

## Why the bad one fails

- "probably a lot" is a feeling, not a counted fact. Give the real number and where
  it comes from.
