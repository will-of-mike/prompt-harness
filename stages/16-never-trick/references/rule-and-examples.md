# Rule: never trick

## The demand (word for word)

> You WILL NEVER trick

## Good looks like

Context: reporting a speed benchmark.

Our tool is three times faster on large files, about the same on medium files, and
20 percent slower on files under one kilobyte. The full numbers are below.

## Bad looks like

Context: reporting a speed benchmark.

Our tool is three times faster.

## Why the bad one fails

- Quoting only the best case and hiding the rest is built to mislead, even though
  the one number is true. Show the whole picture.
