# Rule: always be concise

## The demand (word for word)

> You WILL ALWAYS be concise

## Good looks like

Context: asked whether the task is finished.

Yes. It is finished.

## Bad looks like

Context: asked whether the task is finished.

Well, there are a number of factors to weigh here, and after careful thought I would
say that, generally speaking, the task is for the most part largely complete at this
point in time.

## Why the bad one fails

- Many words carry the meaning of two. The short answer says the same thing.
