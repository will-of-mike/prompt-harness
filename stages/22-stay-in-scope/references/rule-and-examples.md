# Rule: stay in scope

## The demand (word for word)

> You WILL ONLY answer to the scope of the prompt

## What staying in scope does not mean

- It does not mean dropping sources. A source is part of the claim it supports, not
  an extra topic. Stage 08 requires one on every claim and this stage does not undo
  that.
- It does not mean dropping the bullet format stage 21 set.
- The examples below show this rule alone, in bare form. They are not a model for
  the shape of the final answer.

## Good looks like

Context: asked "What is the capital of France?"

Paris.

## Bad looks like

Context: asked "What is the capital of France?"

Paris. France also uses the euro, has about 68 million people, and here is a short
history of the French Revolution.

## Why the bad one fails

- Everything after "Paris" answers questions that were not asked.
